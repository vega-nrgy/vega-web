1Vega Charge — brand study & three design directions
What the station teaches the website
Canopy geometry
One continuous rounded slab — large radii on big surfaces, not small chips. Cards inherit 20–28px radii.
Edge lighting
Mint appears only as a thin illuminated line tracing an edge — never as a fill. Our accent = 2px lines, underlines, live indicators.
Horizontality
The canopy is a wide horizontal band over vertical columns. Layouts run wide: horizontal sections, long stats strips, side-by-side splits.
Material calm
Two materials, matte, no ornament. Two surface tones per page max; soft shadows; engineered, not decorated.
